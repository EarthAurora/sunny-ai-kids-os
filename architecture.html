One platform, with the right data in the right place

The private Operations Hub gives Sunny and the team one working interface. Connected systems hold live records, Obsidian preserves approved organisational knowledge, and AI workers prepare controlled previews across the three layers.

1. Private Operations Hub

One authenticated dashboard for Sunny and authorised team members. It shows only the modules and records each role is allowed to use.

2. Systems of record

CRM, Tutor Hub, WMS, calendar, accounting software and a private database remain authoritative for live transactional records.

3. Obsidian Company Brain

Policies, context, decisions, meeting knowledge, project learning, approved summaries and regular management reports.

Sunny's product decision

The pilot tests whether this connected view matches Sunny's real operating flow and which modules belong in the first production release. This site is a fictional replica. The production copy must be private, require login and enforce database permissions. It must not use GitHub as a database for real client, learner, staff, payroll, bank or accounting records.
