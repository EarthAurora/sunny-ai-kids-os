Sunny AI workforce

The workers appear inside the same operating platform, each supporting a clear business responsibility. “Defined” means its local instructions and safety boundary exist; it does not mean the worker is already connected to a live production database.

Meeting Organiser

Previews

One control model for every worker

Read approved scope → validate the source → separate facts from assumptions → show a preview → obtain human approval → apply only the approved change → record the result.

No worker may silently send a message, publish content, change a deal stage, assign a tutor, approve payment, delete a record or expose private information.
