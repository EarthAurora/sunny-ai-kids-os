Meeting to action

A product-demo view of how a raw meeting becomes a clear decision record, owner-based actions and calendar reminders inside Sunny's connected operating flow. This does not represent a real school, principal, booking, deal or tutor assignment.

1. Raw meeting input

2 September 2026

Sunny meets fictional Principal Ms Grace Chan of fictional Harbour View Primary School.

The discussion confirms a fictional Teachable Machine workshop for 12 September, 10:00–12:00.

2. Confirmed decisions

David Kim is the fictional assigned tutor. The workshop is planned at Harbour View Primary School.

Open items are the fictional student number and room-equipment requirements.

3. Action tasks

  • David Kim: update teaching materials and send them to Sunny by 9 September.
  • Sunny: confirm the fictional CRM booking and move the fictional deal to Won by 3 September.

4. Calendar output

Two deadline reminders are produced from the approved action items.
