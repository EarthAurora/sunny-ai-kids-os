Does this OS fit Sunny's business?

Review the pilot by business outcome. The goal is to identify what the first private production version must do, what can wait and where human approval is essential.

Privacy note: This page does not submit or save responses online. Use fictional or general comments only. Print or save the completed page as a PDF if Sunny wants to keep the discussion record.

1. Clients and sales

Can the team see every enquiry, next action, proposal, confirmed programme and deal outcome without losing context?

2. Tutors and staffing

Can Sunny match the right tutor, see availability, confirm preparation and review schedules and work records?

3. Programmes and calendar

Can the team move from a confirmed deal to a scheduled class with clear ownership, deadlines and delivery status?

4. Materials and devices

Can staff confirm that stationery, art supplies, handicraft materials, painting supplies and iPads are ready?

5. Meetings and follow-up

Can decisions reliably become approved actions with an owner, deadline and reminder?

6. Marketing

Can the team prepare bilingual social content in Sunny's tone using verified programme facts and an approval step?

7. Finance and management

Can Sunny receive a useful weekly and monthly view of sales, delivery, tutor capacity, stock, projects and finance exceptions?

8. Access and approvals

Is it clear what Sunny, administrators, tutors and marketing staff may see or change, and which actions require approval?
